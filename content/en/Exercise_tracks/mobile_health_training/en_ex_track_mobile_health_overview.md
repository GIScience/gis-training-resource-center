# Exercise Track: Designing a mobile health programme in Colombia

## Overview 

This exercise track focuses on assessing the viability of proposed mobile health unit (MHU) stops in the Colombian border region with Venezuela. The exercise track has two parallel tracks focussing on two different expertise profiles: __Track A__ is aimed at professionals working in Information Management and aims to teach how IM can support professionals working in the health sector. It requires a solid understanding of GIS. __Track B__ focuses on Health professionals and teaches them the basics of GIS with practical examples in their field. The exercise track is designed to cover two entire days, where both teams will complete the exercises in parallel. At the end of each day, the groups can present their findings and results.
